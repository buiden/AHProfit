# Changelog

## 0.6.1
- Guild sharing, skip fix: a member skipped for sending nothing is tried again at the next regular ask (login, hourly, after a cut), and after 1 hour at most, so an honest member who reloaded or dropped briefly is no longer left out for the whole life of its scan. No skip is recorded while sharing is off or outside a guild. Only a chunk of a transfer whose header was heard, or a header of this version, shows the picked member is sending. At equal scan times a member ready to send now is picked first; never an older scan for that.
- Guild sharing, second review: Q answers and picks now use the same per-scan window, starting when the header is successfully sent, not when it is queued. Any broadcast of that scan clears its remembered request. Asks received after the header are distinguished by local order, even within one server second; each election keeps at most 50 offers. Exception for reload recovery: requests remembered during the automatic share after a fresh scan are served once immediately after it finishes, then the 10-minute window restarts from that re-send. This allows at most two automatic broadcasts of a fresh scan in its first approximately 10 minutes; answer broadcasts keep the window rule. Player-requested `/ahp share` remains an explicit override.
- Scan progress window: while a full auction house scan runs, a small movable window shows the items so far against the last full scan's count (a plain bar on the first scan), "keep the auction house open", and "paused while you sell" or "waiting for the server". It says "finished: N items, shared with your guild" (or "sharing off") and hides 5 seconds later. When a scan stops early it says why, in the window and in chat: the auction house was closed, a search replaced it (any auction house search not sent by the scan: yours, a filter such as a level range, or another addon's; a scan that has one is never counted complete), the server refused it, or it ended short ("only 1,100 of about 5,200 items"); the reason stays until you close the window or open the auction house again. Nothing changes in what counts as a complete scan. `/ahp progress on|off|reset`; the window's position is saved (`AHProfitDB.progress`, `AHProfitDB.progressPos`, both optional). It sits on UIParent and never touches the auction house window. Favorites searches are not watched: a Favorites answer is rejected unless it covers half the house (the usual completeness rule). A search sent just before a scan delays the scan's own query until it is answered, 3 seconds at most, so its answer is not taken for the scan.
- Guild sharing, review fixes: a transfer is cut only 15 minutes after its last chunk, not its header, so a big scan that takes longer than 15 minutes to send arrives instead of being asked for again and again. Offers heard in the 5 seconds before an ask count in it, so two members asking a second or two apart pick the same owner. The "cut by a reload" line and its ask come once per cut transfer (not at every login), and not at all when your own complete scan is at least as new (saved as the optional `AHProfitDB.cutSaid`). The ask at login waits when a transfer is already coming in. When nobody offers, the asker asks once the old way, so members still on 0.6.0 can answer (at most once per 10 minutes). The "receiving prices" line comes at most once per member per 10 minutes.
- Guild sharing asks again: about once an hour (55 to 65 minutes, so a guild doesn't ask all at once), and 30 to 40 seconds after a transfer is cut, whether it ran out (15 minutes without the rest) or a `/reload` or logout ended it (seen at login from received prices newer than the last complete guild scan). No ask while a transfer runs either way or within 10 minutes of the last one. A member answers at most once per 10 minutes; someone who asked while a broadcast was running gets it once those 10 minutes are up. Chat says "receiving prices from Name's scan (about N items)..." when a scan starts arriving and when a transfer was cut; `/ahp` shows the next check and the last cut transfer.
- Guild sharing picks one sender: an ask is now a tiny "who has newer prices?" message; every member whose own complete scan is newer and under 12 hours old offers it (scan time and item count, no prices), the asker takes the newest offer (equal times: the lowest name, so every asker picks the same member), and only that member sends its scan. Several members asking at once still get one broadcast. Each scan goes out at most once per 10 minutes; a member who asks inside those 10 minutes (after a `/reload` cut, or logging in just after a broadcast) gets it once when they end, so recovery after a cut takes up to about 10 minutes. If the picked member sends nothing within 30 seconds (plus the wait it announced while inside its 10 minutes), the asker leaves that member and scan out and asks once more at once, so a made-up offer costs one extra round, not an hour. `/ahp` lists the offers heard ("Guild prices available: Name 5m (5,200 items)"). Members on 0.6.0 still get answers to their own requests, but they ignore the new messages, so they are not picked to send until they update. Trust rules, the scan message format and saved data are unchanged.

## 0.6.0
- Bags-full advisor: when the bags are full, a small window suggests merging partial stacks, or the least valuable slot to destroy in a bag the item fits (special bags count only for their own items), with protected categories (quest items, tools, hearthstone, keys, class reagents, food and potions unless grey, equipment sets, your keep list) never suggested, and a loot comparison when a drop does not fit. `/ahp keep|unkeep <item>`, `/ahp bagfull <0-20>|off`. Advice only: nothing is deleted, moved or sold. Saves only the keep list (up to 500 ids), the threshold and the window position.
- Gold ledger, session archive and item names (saved data version 3): money in and out per character, day and source (vendor, repair, auction house including auction sales taken from the mailbox, mail, trade, trainer, flight, quest, loot, other), kept 180 days for up to 50 characters; each stopped farming session archived (last 100) with zone, time, coin by source, items and nodes; names and vendor prices of bank, auction and session items (up to 5,000). Data only, for tools outside the game.
- Auctionator as an optional price source: when neither you nor the guild has scanned an item, or the newest scan is more than 24 hours old, a price Auctionator saw today is used (through its public API, every call protected; nothing changes without Auctionator), unless a complete scan of yours or the guild's since Auctionator's day began did not have the item. Shown as "Auctionator, today", with no listed count. `/ahp auctionator on|off`, on by default.
- Farm window: items for which the addon's own price (the one the tooltip shows) is newer than the bundled farm data use that newer auction price (gathering, mobs, fishing and crafting). Without a newer price the numbers are unchanged.
- Daily price history from your own scans: every search and full scan records, per item and day, the highest and lowest of the lowest prices and the most listed (21 days, up to 10,000 items per auction house; guild prices are not recorded). Deals measure "well under the usual price" against your own history once it covers 5 days, else the bundled history, and say which. Saved data: `AHProfitDB.v = 2`, `AHProfitDB.hist` (one compact string per item, about 1 MB for 6,000 items over 21 days; a large scan is written over several frames, each row to the house and day it was scanned on; an item with no day left in the last 21 gives its place to a new one).
- Daily price history: a row older than the item's newest recorded day (seen before midnight and written after, or after the server clock stepped back) goes in day order instead of starting the item's history over; a history with a day after today is kept as it is.
- Guild sharing: received prices are kept in their own layer and never overwrite or delete your own scans. Your own scan wins while it is under 2 hours old, whatever the bundled snapshot's age; after that a newer guild scan is shown, as `guild (Name, 20m ago)`. Scans timed more than 5 minutes ahead or more than 12 hours behind your clock are refused, only your own scans are passed on, received prices older than 12 hours are dropped, a complete newer guild scan replaces the older received prices, and the shared layer is capped (60,000 items, 8 auction houses). Saved data: `AHProfitDB.v = 1`, `AHProfitDB.shared`.
